
# Prompt Engineering Comparison

## Objective
Compare Zero-Shot and Few-Shot prompting techniques
for IT support ticket classification.

## Evaluation Dataset
First 5 tickets from tickets.csv.

## Results

| Prompt Technique | Correct Predictions | Accuracy |
|---|---:|---:|
| Zero-Shot | 4/5 | 80% |
| Few-Shot | 5/5 | 100% |

## Selected Approach
Few-Shot Prompting.

## Reason
Few-Shot prompting produced better classification
results on the five evaluated tickets.

## Conclusion
Few-Shot prompting was selected for the
classify_ticket() function.
