# 1. Password Management

Employees of TechNova Solutions must follow the company password security policy to protect their accounts and confidential information.

## Password Requirements

1. Passwords must contain at least 12 characters.
2. Passwords must include uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters.
3. Employees must change their passwords every 90 days.
4. The last 5 passwords cannot be reused.
5. Employees should never share passwords with colleagues or IT support staff.
6. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) must be enabled for all employee accounts.

## Password Reset Procedure

If an employee forgets their password, they can reset it using the company self-service password portal.

Steps:

1. Open the company password portal at https://password.technova.example.
2. Click the "Forgot Password" option.
3. Enter your registered company email address.
4. Complete identity verification using your registered MFA device.
5. Follow the password reset instructions.
6. Create a new password following the company password requirements.
7. Log in again using the new password.

The password reset process normally takes 2 to 5 minutes.

## Common Password Errors

- Error 101: Incorrect username or password.
- Error 102: Password expired.
- Error 103: Account temporarily locked.
- Error 104: MFA verification failed.

If an account is locked, employees must wait 15 minutes before trying again or contact the IT Helpdesk.

If the reset portal is unavailable for more than 10 minutes, employees should raise an IT support ticket.

## Security Guidelines

Employees must never send passwords through email or chat. IT support staff will never ask employees to reveal their passwords. If an employee suspects that their account has been compromised, they should immediately report the incident to the IT Helpdesk.

# 2. VPN Support

TechNova Solutions provides a Virtual Private Network (VPN) service that allows employees to securely access internal company resources when working remotely.

## VPN Connection Requirements

Employees must install the approved TechNova Secure VPN application on their laptops. A stable internet connection with a minimum speed of 5 Mbps is recommended.

The VPN server address is vpn.technova.example.

Employees must authenticate using their company username, password, and registered MFA device.

## VPN Connection Procedure

1. Open the TechNova Secure VPN application.
2. Enter vpn.technova.example as the server address.
3. Enter your company username.
4. Enter your account password in the VPN application.
5. Complete MFA verification.
6. Click the Connect button.
7. Wait until the application displays Connected.

A successful VPN connection normally takes 10 to 30 seconds.

## Common VPN Errors

### Error: Connection Timed Out

This error occurs when the VPN application cannot establish a connection with the company VPN server.

Troubleshooting steps:

1. Check whether your internet connection is working.
2. Disconnect and reconnect your Wi-Fi.
3. Restart the VPN application.
4. Verify the server address.
5. Try connecting again after 30 seconds.

If the problem continues for more than 5 minutes, raise an IT support ticket.

### Error: Authentication Failed

This error usually occurs because of incorrect login credentials or an MFA problem.

Employees should verify their username and check their registered MFA device.

### VPN Keeps Disconnecting

If the VPN repeatedly disconnects, employees should check internet stability, restart the VPN application, and record the approximate disconnection time.

If the issue continues for more than 10 minutes, employees should raise a VPN support ticket.

## VPN Security Rules

Employees must not share VPN credentials with anyone. Public Wi-Fi should be avoided when accessing confidential company resources. Employees must disconnect the VPN when it is no longer required.

# 3. Email and Outlook Support

TechNova Solutions uses Microsoft Outlook for official employee communication. All employees receive a company email account.

## Mailbox Information

The standard employee mailbox storage limit is 10 GB.

The maximum attachment size is 25 MB per email.

Deleted emails remain in the Deleted Items folder for 30 days before automatic removal.

Employees should regularly delete unnecessary emails and archive old messages to prevent mailbox storage problems.

## Outlook Setup

Employees can access company email through Microsoft Outlook or the approved company webmail portal.

To configure Outlook:

1. Open Microsoft Outlook.
2. Select Add Account.
3. Enter your official company email address.
4. Complete company authentication.
5. Approve the MFA request.
6. Wait for Outlook to synchronize emails.

Initial synchronization normally takes 1 to 3 minutes.

## Common Outlook Problems

### Outlook Not Opening

If Outlook does not open:

1. Close Outlook completely.
2. Open Task Manager.
3. Check whether Outlook is running in the background.
4. End the Outlook process if necessary.
5. Restart Outlook.

If the application still does not open, raise an IT support ticket.

### Mailbox Storage Full

If the mailbox reaches the 10 GB storage limit, employees may experience email sending and receiving problems.

Employees should delete unnecessary messages, empty Deleted Items, and archive old emails.

### Email Delivery Delayed

Email delivery may be delayed because of network problems or mail server maintenance.

Employees should wait 5 minutes and check their internet connection. If the email is still not delivered after 15 minutes, contact IT support.

### Suspicious Email or Strange Link

Employees must not open suspicious attachments or enter credentials on unknown websites.

If an employee accidentally clicks a suspicious link, they must immediately report the incident to the IT Helpdesk by raising a security-related email support ticket.

## Email Security Guidelines

Employees must never share confidential company information with unknown recipients. Suspicious emails should be reported immediately. IT support will never request an employee's account password through email.
